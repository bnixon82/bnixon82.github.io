The Heart Behind the Brand

Hi, I’m Bee. So glad you're here.

I’m the founder and creative heart behind All Things Bee — a greeting card brand rooted in encouragement, connection, creativity and the belief that the right words can stay with someone long after the moment has passed.

This brand is deeply personal to me, because it brings together so many of the things I love most: people, meaningful words, beautiful details and creating something that makes someone feel seen.

With love, Bee
How it began

The words were always there.

I have always been the encouraging friend, the one who sends the message, checks in, celebrates the win and tries to find the words when someone else cannot.

I am usually the friend reminding someone that a hard season will not last forever, telling them to keep going or celebrating their accomplishment as if it were my own.

Over time, I began to realize just how much those words mattered. Sometimes one thoughtful message can change the way someone walks into the rest of their day.

That realization became the heart of All Things Bee.

I wanted to create cards that feel more personal than something quickly picked from a shelf; cards that feel warm, thoughtful and intentional. Cards that encourage us, celebrate us and remind us that we are seen.

Another side of Bee

Technology by profession. Creativity by nature.

Before All Things Bee became part of my story, I spent more than 22 years working in Enterprise Information Technology, supporting some of the world’s largest Fintech companies.

My career has taught me a great deal about leadership, communication, resilience, problem-solving and the importance of showing up for people.

But All Things Bee has given me something very different: a creative outlet.

After years of working in highly structured, technical environments, this brand allows me to lean into another side of myself: writing, designing, imagining, choosing colors, shaping ideas and creating thoughtful details from beginning to end.

All Things Bee is where my love of words, people, creativity and beautiful details finally found a home.
Two worlds, one story

Structure and creativity can live in the same place.

My technology career taught me how to think critically, communicate clearly and build with intention.

All Things Bee gives me the freedom to take those same qualities and use them in a completely different way; to build something warm, creative and deeply personal.

In many ways, this brand is where both sides of me finally meet.

My why

Because people deserve to feel seen.

I believe encouragement matters. Celebration matters. Checking in matters. Saying “I’m proud of you,” “I’m thinking of you,” or “keep going” matters.

Life moves quickly, and it is easy to assume the people we love already know how we feel. All Things Bee is my way of helping us slow down long enough to actually say it.

I also wanted to create a brand where the people receiving the cards could see themselves reflected in the beauty, joy, strength and everyday moments being celebrated.

My hope is that opening an All Things Bee card feels a little like receiving a hug.
