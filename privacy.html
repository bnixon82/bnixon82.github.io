Privacy Policy

Your privacy matters to us. This policy explains what information we collect through our website and how we use it.

Last updated: October 6, 2026

1. Information We Collect

We may collect personal information that you voluntarily provide when you interact with the All Things Bee website.

  • Your first name
  • Your email address
  • Information submitted through our contact form
  • Messages, questions or comments you choose to send us

2. How We Use Your Information

We use the information you provide to communicate with you, respond to inquiries, manage newsletter signups and share information about All Things Bee.

  • Responding to questions or messages
  • Sending newsletter or mailing-list communications
  • Sharing product updates and new releases
  • Sending encouraging content and brand updates
  • Improving the website and overall customer experience

3. Email Communications

If you join our mailing list, you may receive occasional emails from All Things Bee containing brand news, card releases, encouraging messages and promotional information.

You may unsubscribe from these communications at any time by using the unsubscribe option provided in an email or by contacting us.

4. Contact Forms and Formspree

All Things Bee may use Formspree to process information submitted through website forms. When you submit a form, the information you provide may be transmitted to and processed by Formspree in order to deliver the submission to us.

Formspree operates under its own privacy practices and policies. We encourage you to review the privacy practices of third-party services you use.

5. Third-Party Links

Our website may contain links to third-party websites or platforms, including Instagram. All Things Bee is not responsible for the privacy practices, content or security of third-party websites.

6. Cookies and Website Data

At this time, All Things Bee does not intentionally use advertising cookies or sell personal information collected through the website. However, third-party services used in connection with the website may use their own technologies and data practices.

7. How We Protect Information

We take reasonable steps to protect information submitted through the website. However, no method of online transmission or electronic storage can be guaranteed to be completely secure.

8. Sharing of Personal Information

All Things Bee does not sell or rent personal information to advertisers or unrelated third parties.

Information may be shared with service providers only when reasonably necessary to operate the website or provide requested services.

9. Your Choices

You may contact All Things Bee to request that we update or remove personal information you previously submitted through the website.

You may also unsubscribe from marketing communications at any time.

10. Changes to This Policy

We may update this Privacy Policy from time to time as the All Things Bee website and business evolve. Any updated policy will be posted on this page with a revised effective date.

11. Contact Us

If you have questions about this Privacy Policy or how your information is handled, please contact us through our Contact page.
