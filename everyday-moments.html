No occasion required

Sometimes “I thought of you” is enough.

Not every meaningful card needs a birthday, holiday or milestone attached to it. Some of the best words arrive unexpectedly — simply because someone matters.

A little note with a lot of meaning

Thinking of You.

Sometimes there is no big announcement and nothing specific to fix. You simply want someone to know they crossed your mind.

Everyday Moments is being created for those quiet connections — the friend you miss, the person you appreciate, the family member who could use a smile or someone you simply want to remind, “you matter to me.”

“You crossed my mind today, and I wanted you to know.”

Simple words. Genuine connection. No special occasion necessary.

The little things are often the big things

For connection that does not need a reason.

Everyday Moments celebrates the relationships and feelings that live between life's major milestones — the kind of connection that deserves to be acknowledged simply because it exists.
