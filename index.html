The heart behind the brand

Hi, I’m Bee.

I created All Things Bee from a love of meaningful words and the belief that encouragement can arrive at exactly the moment someone needs it.

I wanted to create greeting cards that feel personal, intentional and warm — cards that reflect us, celebrate us and remind us of our strength.

Whether someone is celebrating a milestone, navigating something difficult or simply needs to know that they are loved, I hope every All Things Bee card feels like a small hug they can hold onto.

With love, Bee
